# Privacy Policy — Job Skills Matcher

**Last updated: June 2026**

---

## Overview

Job Skills Matcher is a browser extension that helps job seekers assess their fit for a role by comparing their skills or CV against a job description. This privacy policy explains how the extension handles your data.

**The short version: we collect nothing. All processing happens on your device.**

---

## Data Collection

Job Skills Matcher does not collect, transmit, store on any server, or share any user data — including but not limited to:

- Your CV or resume content
- Your skills or experience
- Job descriptions you analyze
- Match scores or results
- Browsing history or activity
- Personal information of any kind

---

## How Your Data Is Processed

All analysis is performed entirely on your device using an AI model that is downloaded once and runs locally in your browser. No text you enter — including your CV, skills, or job descriptions — is ever sent to an external server or third party.

---

## Local Storage

The extension uses your browser's local storage to save your skills and CV data between sessions, so you do not need to re-enter them each time. This data:

- Never leaves your device
- Is not accessible to the developer or any third party
- Can be cleared at any time using the **Clear / Edit** button in the extension, or by clearing your browser's extension storage in Chrome settings

---

## AI Model Download

To perform on-device analysis, the extension downloads an AI model from [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co) and supporting libraries from [jsDelivr](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net) on first use. These are standard model weight files and open-source libraries. No user data is transmitted during or after this process.

---

## Third Parties

Job Skills Matcher does not:

- Sell or transfer user data to third parties
- Use user data for purposes unrelated to its core function
- Use any analytics, tracking, or advertising services
- Use cookies

---

## Children's Privacy

This extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from any user.

---
